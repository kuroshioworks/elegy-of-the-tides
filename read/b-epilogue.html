Ending B · Sorrow — Epilogue

The Foam-Shadow of Dawn, and a Final Promise

From the moment Owen dissolved into foam and vanished, David gave up everything.

His name. The glory he'd built as a pirate king. Even the crew who had once sailed beside him through it all.

Alone, aboard a battered little boat that carried nothing but the memory of that one presence, he wandered an endless sea that never gave him any answer.

His beard had grown ragged, his cheeks hollow and sunken.

Nothing remained of the man once called the pirate king.

He had searched the ends of the earth, exhausted every scrap of merfolk legend there was to find.

But no matter how far he pushed through the waves, all that ever answered him was the hollow sound of the sea wind.

"…Where are you, Owen…"

On a deck swept by cold night wind, David laid his body down, worn past its limits, and drew a small music box from his pocket.

The old, slightly out-of-tune music box Owen had once found so amusing, and kept so carefully.

With trembling fingers, he wound the key, and a thin, sorrowful, crystalline melody rang out into the night sea.

David let his heavy eyelids fall shut, chasing—again tonight—a beloved shadow he knew could never be reached.

—And then, softly, warmth touched his cheek.

"…Honestly. You're as stubborn as ever, aren't you, David."

A voice there was no mistaking. That achingly familiar voice.

David's eyes flew open, and there, silhouetted against the moonlight, was Owen, looking down at him with an expression somewhere between exasperation and tenderness.

"Owen…?! Is it— is it really you…?! You came back to me…?!"

With no room left to wonder whether this was a dream or an illusion, David wanted, more than anything, to believe that the real Owen had finally returned to him.

He reached out with trembling hands to pull that body into a fierce embrace, but Owen gently caught both his hands and held him back, softly restraining him.

His skin seemed almost translucent.

And yet his hands were, without question, warm.

"…Stop searching, David. I'm not anywhere anymore. However far you wander this vast sea, you'll never find me."

"That's a lie…! But you're right here in front of me! I can touch you—you're warm! So why…!"

At David's desperate cry, Owen's eyes narrowed with sorrow, and quietly, he confessed his secret.

"…It's because you wind this music box and call my name with a face like you're dying. My soul, dissolved into the sea, gathered itself for just one night—only to say goodbye to you. This is the last miracle, lasting only until the key winds down."

"I won't accept that…! I'll keep searching…! However many times you disappear, I'll pull you back, again and again…!"

Half out of his mind, David reached for him, and Owen held him close instead, gently, as if handling something fragile enough to break.

His beloved breath, close against David's ear.

And at their feet, the music box's gentle melody kept softly playing.

"Thank you. …But listen, David. I don't want you wandering this sea with a face that broken."

Owen drew back and gave a small, almost bashful smile.

"…Hey, David. Did you… want to see me?"

"Of course I did…! I wanted to see you—wanted it so badly I thought I'd lose my mind…!"

Unable to hold back the feeling overflowing inside him, David cupped Owen's face in both hands and forced the words out, like something torn straight from his chest.

"…I love you!! With everything I have, with my whole life—it's only ever been you, Owen…!!"

Owen's eyes went wide for an instant, and in the next, fat tears spilled down his cheeks.

"Ha… that's not fair. Say something like that, and I won't want to leave."

"Then don't! Stay with me, always…!"

"…Idiot."

With hands trembling from love, Owen wound his arms around David's neck, leaned in close, and pressed his lips to David's—gently, but with a love so deep it ached.

A sweet, sorrowful kiss—a miracle that would last only this one night.

"Go back to land, David. …Live out the life of the man who loved me, properly, to the end. That's my last wish for your happiness."

"Owen… wait… please, don't go…!"

"Goodbye, David. I love you too."

The next instant, David's outstretched hand closed uselessly around empty air.

The key wound fully down, and the music box's melody fell quietly silent.

At that same moment, the light of dawn broke over the horizon, and Owen's body dissolved, softly, silently, into motes of light within the morning mist.

The last thing he showed was a smile—dazzlingly bright, after wiping away his tears.

What remained was only the gentle sound of the waves, and the faint warmth still lingering on David's lips.

And the music box, now fallen silent.

But the warmth he had received within that "last miracle" had lit a flame once more in David's heart, one he had thought long dead.

Several days later—

A small boat, having drifted the endless sea, quietly washed ashore.

Stepping down from the boat, the former pirate king took the broken compass—the one that had not moved once since that day—and threw it quietly into the sea.

In its place, he closed his hand gently around the music box in his breast pocket.

He looked up at the sky and drew a deep breath. Deep in his chest, that warmth, that melody, remained—something that would never fade for as long as he lived.

(…Wait for me, Owen. Once I've lived out my life—loved it fully, to the very end—I'll come find you.)

Holding the final promise made with the man he had loved close to his heart, David never once looked back, and stepped forward into the land where a new light was breaking.
