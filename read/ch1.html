Chapter One

The Prince of Foam and the Pirate in Black

Captain David was a legend. He ruled the Seven Seas, taking every treasure he desired by force.

On the night of a full moon, his massive black-sailed ship—the Black Grouse—dropped anchor in the shallow waters of Ludwig, the hidden kingdom of the merfolk, veiled in an endless shroud of enchanted mist.

His crew believed they had come in search of the kingdom's legendary treasure, the Tear of the Azure Sky.

But the treasure itself had never been David's true objective.

What he sought was something no one had ever touched.

The purest… …and most beautiful thing in the world.

Leaving his crew behind, David climbed into a small rowboat and headed alone for an ancient temple rising from the shallows. His long black leather coat fluttered behind him in the sea breeze.

Silence filled the sanctuary.

Moonlight streamed through the ruined ceiling, illuminating the crystal-clear water below.

Someone was there.

Adorned with pearls and delicate seashells, a young merman rested near the surface, his face tilted toward the moonlit sky.

It was Owen, the prince of the merfolk.

His sapphire-blue tail drifted beneath the water, each scale catching the moonlight like a polished jewel.

Golden hair shimmered beneath the silver glow.

Defiant blue eyes.

Smooth skin beaded with droplets of water.

There was something undeniably sacred about him.

So breathtakingly beautiful that even time itself seemed to stand still.

"…Who's there?!"

The instant he sensed killing intent from the shadows, Owen's sharp gaze snapped toward the darkness.

A man stepped into the moonlight.

A greatsword rested at one hip.

Twin pistols hung at the other.

Cold.

Silent.

Captain David.

"I came looking for the merfolk's legendary treasure…"

His icy gaze lingered over Owen's shimmering blue scales…

The pearls against his pale skin…

Then settled on those fearless eyes glaring back at him.

Silence fell.

For the briefest instant…

Something long frozen inside David shattered.

…There you are.

The most beautiful jewel I've spent my life searching for.

Without the slightest hesitation, David thrust his arm into the water.

His hand closed firmly around Owen's gleaming blue tail.

"W-What the— Let go!" Owen thrashed violently. "Who the hell do you think you are?! Don't just grab people, you idiot!"

The moment David touched him, panic exploded through Owen's body.

Never once had a human laid a hand on him.

Fear and anger surged together as he lashed his tail wildly, sending water crashing across the temple floor.

David didn't budge.

Instead, he gripped the back of Owen's head and drew him closer until barely a breath separated them.

He studied Owen's face in silence.

"…That saves me the trouble of searching."

His voice was calm.

"You are the greatest treasure this sea has to offer."

"What…? What the hell are you ta—"

The rest of Owen's protest disappeared beneath David's lips.

The kiss was fierce.

Deep enough to steal the air from his lungs.

Hot. Possessive. Unlike anything that existed beneath the sea.

Owen's thoughts went blank.

Before he could even fight back, David lifted him effortlessly into his arms and carried him toward the Black Grouse.

"Screw you, you damn pirate!" Owen shouted, twisting desperately in David's grasp. "Take me back to the ocean! Put me down!"

Only the empty night answered him.

His cries dissolved into the endless sea.

The moment David stepped onto the deck of the Black Grouse, he unceremoniously dropped Owen onto the weathered wooden planks.

"Let me go, you damn pirate!"

Owen snarled, his sapphire-blue tail lashing violently across the deck. "Take me back to the ocean!"

But David wasn't the only one waiting for him.

"Well, I'll be damned!" one pirate boomed with a hearty laugh.

"Now that's what I call treasure!"

"A blue-tailed merman?" another exclaimed. "I've never seen one in my life! Captain really outdid himself this time!"

Towering figures closed in around Owen one by one.

They were David's crew—hulking pirates with arms like tree trunks and bodies built like wild beasts.

Grinning shamelessly, they circled the captured prince, openly admiring the rare spectacle before them.

"W-What the hell are you gorillas staring at?!" Owen snapped, baring his sharp fangs. His powerful tail slammed against the deck with a deafening crack as he hissed, "Back off! Take one more step and I'll bite your damn heads off!"

The pirates only laughed harder.

"Ha! He's even cute when he's threatening us!" "Those fangs actually look dangerous!"

"…Enough."

David's quiet voice sliced cleanly through the laughter.

"Keep your eyes off what's mine."

Silence fell.

Every grin vanished at once.

"…Heh. Guess the captain's taken a real shine to this one." "Yeah… We'd better leave them alone." "Take good care of your treasure, Captain."

Still chuckling under their breath, the pirates quickly dispersed, leaving the deck eerily silent once more.

David reached into his coat and withdrew a small crystal vial filled with a perfectly clear liquid.

Without a word, he removed the stopper and swallowed its contents.

Owen frowned.

"…What are you—"

Before he could finish, David caught the back of his head once again.

"W-Wait… what are you— mmph?!"

Their lips met for the second time. This kiss was different. Something warm flowed from David's mouth into Owen's.

Sweet. Rich. Almost intoxicating.

The liquid slipped past Owen's lips before he could stop it.

His throat betrayed him, swallowing on instinct.

By the time he realized what had happened, every last drop had already disappeared into his body.

Only then did David slowly draw back.

For a fleeting instant, their breaths still mingled between them.

Owen gasped, struggling to catch his breath.

"Hah… damn you…" His chest rose and fell rapidly. "What the hell… did you make me drink…?"

David said nothing.

Instead— Heat.

A fierce, unbearable heat surged through every inch of Owen's body.

His sapphire tail began to glow.

Soft golden light spread across each shimmering scale, wrapping it in an ethereal radiance.

One by one…

The brilliant blue scales dissolved into countless motes of light.

The powerful tail that had carried him effortlessly through the sea slowly vanished.

In its place…

Two long, slender human legs emerged from the fading glow.

"…What…?"

Owen stared down in stunned disbelief. Strong thighs. Lean calves. Feet.

Human feet.

Something he had never possessed in all his life.

The graceful power of his tail was gone.

In its place was an unfamiliar human body—vulnerable, exposed, and so exquisitely sensitive that even the slightest touch felt as though it might make him tremble.

The strands of pearls still draped across his chest.

Moonlight shimmered across his damp skin, lending him an almost ethereal beauty.

"I've given you legs."

David's calm voice broke the silence.

"Legs that can walk on land."

His icy gaze settled on Owen.

"So don't even think about running."

He stepped closer.

"You'll spend the rest of your life where I can always keep my eyes on you."

"In your dreams, you idiot!"

Owen shot back without hesitation.

Furious, he shoved himself upright. The instant he tried to stand— His knees buckled beneath him.

"W-Whoa…?!"

Like a newborn fawn taking its very first steps, his legs refused to obey him.

He pitched forward—

—and collided squarely with David's broad chest.

David didn't so much as flinch.

Without a word, he slipped one powerful arm around Owen's trembling body, drawing him firmly against his chest.

His embrace left no possibility of escape.

Around that same time.

At the shallow-water temple in Ludwig, the hidden kingdom of the merfolk—

Morgan, Owen's childhood friend and commander of the kingdom's elite knights, felt a sharp, piercing unease strike his chest.

Gripping the trident in his hand, he raced to the temple. What awaited him there was a surface violently disturbed, and Owen's blue scales scattered across the seabed.

"…Owen…! Owen!!"

He called the name again and again, reaching out toward the water, but only the quiet murmur of the waves answered him.

Morgan dropped to one knee and pressed a fallen scale tightly into his palm.

He had trained himself half to death in distant seas, had returned as the strongest of knights, for one reason above all others—to protect Owen.

"…I will take him back. No matter what."

When Morgan lifted his head, the warmth that always filled his eyes had vanished, replaced by the cold, fierce resolve of a commander.

He returned to the palace at once and rang the emergency bells, summoning the kingdom's elite merman forces.

Before the ranks of armed merfolk soldiers standing at attention, Morgan raised his trident and let his voice ring out low and clear.

"Hear me, all forces! Our prince, Lord Owen, has been taken by a human pirate!"

"What—?!"

"It can't be…!"

"The pirate's ship still lies somewhere in these waters. By the pride of the Sea God, and by my spear—we will bring the prince home, safe and unharmed!"

A thunderous roar rose from the assembled warriors.

With a cheer like rolling thunder, the merfolk army surged upward from the seabed toward the surface, sapphire tails streaming behind them in unison.

With Morgan at their head, the relentless pursuit of the pirate ship Black Grouse had begun.

(Wait for me, Owen… No matter what it takes, I will take you back from that pirate.)

As Morgan gazed out across the sea lit by the coming dawn, his eyes followed, unwavering, the image of the beloved friend he had grown up beside.
