Chapter Four

The Tide's Elegy

Weeks had passed since they'd left the port town.

On a night when moonlight spilled through the cabin window, Owen sat alone on the bed, gazing quietly out at the sea.

The medicine David had given him to turn a merman into a human kept its effect as long as he stayed out of the water—but it came with one condition: to maintain that effect, he had to regularly receive the antidote through a kiss, passed mouth to mouth from David.

Believing this to be true, Owen found his heart wavering every time he looked down at his own legs—torn between the sweet bond he shared with David and thoughts of where he truly belonged.

(…What am I even doing.)

The sea back home, in Ludwig.

Everyone there must be worried sick.

Morgan, his childhood friend, was surely searching desperately for him even now.

He had a responsibility—as a prince—to return to his kingdom.

And yet—when he looked at David's stern, finely carved profile beside him, breathing quietly in sleep, his chest ached as if it might tear apart.

(I should want to go home… so why can't I bear to leave his side…)

Torn by the conflict inside him, Owen let a quiet tear slip free.

At that moment, David's large hand reached out through the darkness and wiped the wetness from Owen's cheek.

"…You're awake."

"…! David…"

David rose quietly, pulled Owen close, and folded him into his chest.

"…Do you miss home?"

"…! It's nothing…"

Owen tried to sound tough, but David saw straight through him.

As a pirate, his possessiveness alone could have kept Owen locked aboard this ship for the rest of his life.

He had no intention whatsoever of letting him go.

But watching Owen's heart break, watching him cry—that was its own kind of agony for David.

"…I have no intention of giving you up. But if it's causing you this much pain, then I'll find a way—some way for Ludwig and us pirates to coexist. A way that lets you move freely between your kingdom and me."

"Wha—? David, you're… you're a pirate…"

Owen looked up at him, stunned.

That merciless, unfeeling pirate king was trying to meet him halfway.

"If it means sparing you tears, I'll rewrite the laws of the world itself if I have to. …So stop making that face."

"…! David… you idiot…!"

Owen threw his arms tightly around David's neck and buried his face in his chest.

It was the moment their hearts, in the truest sense, became one.

But as if to shatter that peace, a violent shock rocked the ship's hull.

"Captain!! Something's wrong!!"

David and Owen burst out onto the deck.

There, trident raised and sharp, sapphire fin lashing fiercely through the air, stood the merman knight—Morgan.

"Owen…! I finally found you! I've come to save you!"

"Morgan…?!"

"Get away from that pirate, Owen! We're going home, right now!"

"Wait, Morgan! I—!"

Cutting off his words, Morgan lunged forward, his trident wreathed in a current of water. David drew his greatsword and caught the blow head-on.

CLANG!

Steel met spirit-force, sparks scattering across the deck.

"Pirate. The crime of deceiving Owen and dragging him onto land is not a small one."

Morgan's trident thrust forward like a gale, aimed straight at David's throat. David sidestepped it by a hair's breadth, deflected the spear with the flat of his blade, and fixed him with an icy stare.

"I never deceived him. I respect his own will."

"Silence! What could a man who's stolen his duty as prince, and his life in the sea, possibly change now?"

The heavy strikes of the greatsword against the fluid, water-wreathed thrusts of the trident—the two men's unyielding convictions and pride collided in a breathless, deadly duel across the deck.

But then—shattering even that fragile balance—the depths below suddenly churned black and spiraled into a whirlpool.

From it rose something ancient, a legendary monster said to have slept in the deep sea since time immemorial—a Kraken.

Countless massive tentacles, like serpents, rose into the air, whipping up a violent storm as they moved to swallow the Black Grouse and Morgan's army whole in an instant.

"…A Kraken?!"

"Don't you dare lay a hand on Owen, monster!!"

Morgan swung his trident, slicing through tentacles; David cut them down with his greatsword.

"Protect the captain!!" the gorilla-pirates roared, hurling cannon fire, while the merfolk soldiers loosed arrows of rushing water.

Pirates and merfolk.

Two forces that should have been sworn enemies now stood united, fighting together to protect Owen—and for their own survival in a battle for the sea itself.

"Owen, stay behind me!"

"I can fight too!!"

Owen planted his human feet firmly on the deck, gripping a dagger he'd found.

But the monster's fury had reached its peak.

A tentacle the size of a mountain came crashing down from above, straight toward Owen's unguarded head.

"OWEN!!!!!"

Morgan's scream tore through the air.

But the distance was too great. He couldn't reach in time.

"…! I won't let you!"

At that instant, the one who threw himself in front of Owen was—

David.

A brutal impact thundered across the deck.

David shielded him with his greatsword, blocking the tentacle completely—but the monster's venomous barbs and sheer overwhelming mass tore through David's body, hurling him backward.

"DAVIIIIID!!!!"

Owen's scream ripped through the air.

David coughed up blood and collapsed onto the deck.

Venom-laced crimson blood poured from his chest, and the light drained rapidly from the once merciless pirate king's eyes.

"…Are you… unhurt… Owen…"

"You idiot!! What the hell were you thinking!! Why did you protect me?!"

Owen clutched David's body and screamed like something had broken inside him.

Morgan rushed in and, drawing on the last of his strength, drove his trident through the monster in a final blow. With a shriek, the creature sank back into the depths.

The fierce battle over, silence fell across the deck.

But David's life was already slipping away. Venom had spread through his entire body, and his warmth was draining fast.

"No… the poison's spread through his whole body…! No magic can undo this…!"

Morgan went pale, unable to speak.

"WAAAAAH!! Captaaaain!!"

The gorilla-pirates wailed and sobbed.

Tears blurring his vision, Owen gently laid his hand against David's cheek, already turning cold.

"…It's not true… right, David… open your eyes… You said you'd show me the whole world… you said it…"

David's eyes fluttered faintly open, and he murmured in broken fragments.

"…I'm sorry… Owen… it looks like… I won't be able to… take you there… after all…"

"Don't be ridiculous!! Don't you dare give up like that!!"

Owen pressed his hand hard against his own chest and, quietly, made up his mind.

(…And here I said I'd never sing for you.)

Wiping away his tears, Owen leaned his face close to David's chest, and slowly, parted his lips.

"Morgan… David… everyone… thank you, for everything, until now."

"Owen… don't tell me… you're going to—!"

Morgan's eyes went wide.

What rang out, cutting through the sound of rain and the quiet murmur of the waves, was a merman's voice more beautiful, more sacred, more achingly sorrowful than any music this world had ever known.

The instant the song spilled out, golden light wrapped around Owen's body.

His human legs regained their graceful curve, returning to the sapphire-blue fin and scales, shimmering with the same mysterious beauty as the night David had first found him.

At his hair and chest, the shells and pearls that marked him as the merfolk's prince began to glow with an otherworldly light.

"Stop it… Owen!! If a merman sings the elegy of life… it takes your own life with it…!!"

But Morgan's anguished cry no longer reached Owen's ears.

The pure melody wrapped itself around the entire night sea.

With every note of Owen's beloved voice, the venom eating away at David's body dissolved, and his wounds began to close.

Owen's life, his very soul, was pouring directly into David as life force.

"Stop… I'm begging you, don't sing…!"

David's eyes reddened, and great tears spilled down his face.

But Owen only gazed at him with love, and quietly kept singing.

From Owen's blue tail, one beautiful sapphire scale after another dissolved into motes of light, rising into the night sky as sea foam.

"…What—?!"

David stared down at his own hands, whole and moving again, the wounds completely healed—and understood, with despair, exactly what that cruel fact meant.

If his own life had been saved, it could only mean Owen's was about to end.

"Stop… don't sing…! Owen!!"

Breath ragged with terror at the thought of Owen vanishing in exchange for his own life, David wrenched himself upright.

But it was in that very instant—

Having sung the final note, the light went out of Owen's eyes, and like a puppet with its strings cut, he collapsed limply into the water pooling on the deck.

David gathered Owen's fading body into his arms, cradling the smooth, beautiful blue tail carefully in his large hands, as if trying to scoop up something already slipping away.

His dark shadow fell over Owen from above.

Gone limp in David's arms, Owen opened his eyes just a sliver, gazing up through strands of wet golden hair into David's narrow, sharp eyes.

Their gazes met, impossibly close.

"…David."

At that faint call, David's brow twitched.

The tail he held was already dissolving into translucent, glowing foam, and no matter how tightly he wrapped his arms around it, it slipped cruelly through his fingers.

"Don't cry, you damn pirate… I've never seen you make a face like that before."

Owen's trembling fingers brushed David's cheek, already cold as ice.

David set aside, for the first time, the composure that never usually cracked, and pressed his own hand over Owen's.

"…You're growing cold. Don't you dare smile through this."

His voice was low, shaking.

The mind that always calmly analyzed every situation could think of nothing at all right now.

"I wanted to… see the whole world with you…"

"…I'll show you. Wherever you want to go, I'll take you there. So… don't leave me behind. Please… please don't…!!"

David curled his large hand behind Owen's fading head and leaned down, sealing their lips together in one last, deep kiss.

Overflowing tears mixed with sea spray, in a final vow sealed with a kiss.

"…I love you, David."

The moment their lips parted, Owen's body burst into brilliant gold and sapphire light—one beautiful bubble of foam—and dissolved completely from within David's arms.

All that remained in his embrace was the cold seawater of the night, and the small music box they had bought together in that port town.

"Owen…! OWEN…!!!"

The pirate king's scream of despair—his life restored, his heart shattered—echoed on and on across the dawn-lit sea, without end.
